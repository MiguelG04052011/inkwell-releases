# Inkwell — downloads

**Inkwell** is the story bible for comic creators: your heroes, villains,
secret identities, power levels and issues in one place, drawn back as
pictures — the web of who knows whom, Versus, the story grid, and a page
planner laid out the way comics are printed.

👉 **[inkwell-comics.pages.dev](https://inkwell-comics.pages.dev)** — everything about it, and the browser version.

## Download

- **Mac** (macOS 12 Monterey or later, Apple silicon and Intel):
  [Inkwell-mac.dmg](https://github.com/MiguelG04052011/inkwell-releases/releases/latest/download/Inkwell-mac.dmg)
- **Any computer:** [open Inkwell in your browser](https://inkwell-comics.pages.dev/app/)
- **Windows:** coming soon.

The first time you open it on a Mac, macOS asks you to confirm it once —
[here's how](https://inkwell-comics.pages.dev/download/#first-open).

Each release lists the file's SHA-256 fingerprint, so you can check your
download is exactly the one published here:

```
shasum -a 256 ~/Downloads/Inkwell-mac.dmg
```
